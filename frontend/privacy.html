Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains what information WorkBridge ETH collects when you use the platform, why we collect it, and how it's handled. It's written in plain language on purpose — if anything here is unclear, reach out using the contact details at the bottom.

Before you rely on this: this is a genuine, good-faith privacy policy describing what this specific platform actually does — it is not legal advice, and it hasn't been reviewed by a lawyer. If you're operating WorkBridge ETH for real users, have it reviewed against Ethiopian data protection law and the law of any other country your users are in before you depend on it for compliance.

What we collect

When you create an account: your full name, username, email address, phone number, and a password (which we never store in plain text — see "How we protect it" below).

Depending on how you use the platform, you may also add: a profile photo, bio, location (country/region/city), skills, languages, and — for employer, freelance, modeling, or dating profiles specifically — additional details relevant to that section, such as company name, portfolio information, physical attributes for modeling listings, or dating preferences and interests.

We also keep records that the platform needs to function: job postings and applications you submit or receive, messages you send through the platform, service listings and orders, and matches or likes within the dating section.

What we don't do

We don't sell your personal data. We don't show you third-party ads. We don't track you across other websites.

How we use it

  • To create and run your account, and to show your profile to other users where the platform's features require that (e.g. an employer viewing an applicant's profile, or a dating match seeing your profile).
  • To operate core features: matching you with relevant jobs, services, or connections; delivering messages; processing applications.
  • To send account-related email (verification, password resets) — only if the platform operator has configured an email provider.
  • To keep the platform secure: rate-limiting abusive requests, detecting suspicious activity.

How we protect it

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt before storage — we cannot see or recover your actual password, even internally. Sessions use signed tokens (JWT) rather than storing your credentials in the browser. All admin-level actions are logged. If you choose to upload a profile photo, it's stored with Cloudinary, a third-party image hosting service, rather than on our own servers.

Who we share it with

With other users, only as the platform's normal features require — for example, your profile is visible to people you match with, apply to, or message. With service providers that make the platform work: our hosting provider, our database provider, and — only if configured — an email delivery service and Cloudinary for photo storage. We don't share your data with advertisers or data brokers.

Your choices

  • Update your info any time from your dashboard's Profile tab.
  • Deactivate your account yourself, any time, from Settings. This immediately signs you out everywhere and disables sign-in; contact us if you'd like your data fully deleted rather than deactivated.
  • Ask us anything about what we hold on you, or request a copy or deletion of it, using the contact details below.
